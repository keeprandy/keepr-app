# Brand/KAC Operating Model

Keepr's B2B graph exists to create owner utility.

Every B2B feature must eventually help the owner understand the asset, get help, see what is due, contact the right people, approve work, keep history, contribute knowledge, or transfer the complete story to the next owner.

## Core Rule

```text
Brand relationships establish network eligibility.
Asset/KAC relationships establish operational participation.
Permissions determine visibility and actions.
```

Network eligibility does not grant asset access. A dealer can represent a brand without seeing every KAC for that brand. A specific KAC enters the dealer projection only when the KAC has an asset relationship to that dealer.

## V1 Traversal

```text
Organization
  <-> Brand
       -> Model
          -> KAC
             -> Dealer
             -> Owner
```

The same KAC projects differently by relationship:

- OEM/manufacturer: KACs manufactured or built.
- Dealer: KACs assigned, sold, delivered, or serviced.
- Owner: KACs currently or pending owned.
- SYT Admin: complete lifecycle graph when platform authority allows inspection.

## Product KPI Hierarchy

- Activated KACs are supply.
- Activated owners are adoption.
- Returning owners are product-market fit.
- Service interactions are utility.
- Transferred KACs are proof of continuity.

The OEM/dealer graph distributes Keepr. The owner experience is what makes Keepr matter.
